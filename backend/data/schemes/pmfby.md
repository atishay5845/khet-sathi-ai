# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
Source: https://pmfby.gov.in/

PMFBY is a crop insurance programme that provides notified crop and area coverage subject to the applicable season notification, enrolment window, insurance unit, crop details and scheme conditions.

Farmers should check the current state and season notification, notified crops, cut-off dates, premium details, sum insured and claim procedure through the official portal or local agriculture and insurance authorities. Eligibility and coverage cannot be concluded from crop and location alone.

Official portal: https://pmfby.gov.in/

Evidence note: This local reference is an orientation summary. Current seasonal notifications take precedence.
