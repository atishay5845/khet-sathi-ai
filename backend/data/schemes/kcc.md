# Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
Source: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/

The Kisan Credit Card facility is intended to support eligible farmers' access to short-term credit for cultivation and related agricultural needs, subject to lender assessment and applicable banking guidelines.

Applicants should confirm current eligibility, documentation, limits, interest support, renewal terms and application process with a participating bank or official government scheme information. The advisory system must not represent a potential match as loan approval.

Official scheme discovery: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/

Evidence note: This local reference is an orientation summary. The participating bank and current government guidelines determine the applicable terms.
