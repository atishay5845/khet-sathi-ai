# PM-KISAN — Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi
Source: https://pmkisan.gov.in/

PM-KISAN is a central sector scheme for eligible landholding farmer families, subject to exclusions and verification requirements defined by the Government of India. Scheme rules, beneficiary lists and payment status can change; consult the official portal for current conditions.

The scheme provides income support in instalments to eligible beneficiary farmer families. Land records, identity verification, e-KYC and bank account details may be relevant to beneficiary verification. The platform must not infer approval from crop type, location or land area alone.

Official application and beneficiary information: https://pmkisan.gov.in/

Evidence note: This local reference is an orientation summary. Always confirm current rules and eligibility on the official PM-KISAN portal.
